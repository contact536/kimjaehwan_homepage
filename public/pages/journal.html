Methodology & Disclaimer

Journal tiers here refer to JCR Quartiles (Q1–Q4), which rank journals by Impact Factor within their Web of Science subject category:

  • Q1: top 25% within its category · Q2: 25–50% · Q3: 50–75% · Q4: bottom 25%
  • KCI: Korean Citation Index — Korean national indexing status, not a JCR tier.
  • Tiers are NOT based on acceptance rate. Most journals do not publicly report acceptance rates.
  • Impact Factor values listed are from recent JCR editions (2023–2024). Some values may differ slightly from the latest JCR release or are estimates where the journal is newly indexed.

Official indexing sources: JCR (Clarivate) · Scimago Journal & Country Rank · Master Journal List · KCI (Korean)

The "Source" column links to Scimago for each journal — a free public lookup for Impact Factor, quartile, and indexing history.
