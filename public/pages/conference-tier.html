Methodology & Disclaimer

Tiers on this page are not based solely on acceptance rate. They combine multiple sources:

  • User-curated list (63 conferences): community consensus (CORE/CCF-style judgment).
  • Bulk additions (~200 conferences): averaged rankings from Korea's top CS schools (KAIST, SNU, POSTECH), BK21+ IF, and Korean Information Science Society — aggregated in Pusnow's CS Conference Ranking.
  • D-tier additions (~70 conferences): estimated from general community reputation. Acceptance rates marked with "~" are approximations and may not reflect official published numbers.
  • Format (In-person / Hybrid / Online) is inferred from recent editions and may change year to year.

Official ranking sources: CORE · CCF · Research.com · DBLP · Pusnow gist

The "Source" column links to DBLP search for each conference — use it to verify existence, publication record, and recent editions.
